About Me

A Quick Overview

The Bridge Between Business & Technology

I work at the intersection of business, technology, data, and financial services. My background combines software development with business analysis and financial systems, allowing me to understand both the business problem and the technology behind the solution.


I hold dual degrees in Computer Science and Finance and have over four years of professional experience in software development through Tata Consultancy Services. My experience includes working with Java, SQL, Angular, REST APIs, Power BI, and Agile SDLC environments, supporting software development and data-driven solutions.


In financial services, I apply that technical background to CRM systems, financial data management, reporting, system migration, data validation, and process improvement. I work with platforms including Ameriprise CRM, AdvisorCompass, NaviPlan, and MoneyGuide, along with Excel and Power BI to analyze, validate, and manage business data.


What I Do Best

I enjoy understanding how a business process works, identifying where technology or data can improve it, and helping turn that understanding into a practical solution. My technical background allows me to communicate with both business stakeholders and technical teams, while my finance background helps me understand the business context behind financial data and processes.


Technology & Development

On the development side, I work with Java, Spring Boot, Angular, JavaScript, SQL, REST APIs, AWS, and Power BI. I enjoy building applications, working with data, and turning complex requirements into functional digital solutions.


Beyond the Codebase

Outside of work, I enjoy reading, playing guitar, gaming, and building personal projects inspired by the technology and franchises I enjoy.
