About Me

A Quick Overview

The Bridge Between Logic & Capital

Most people see code and finance as two different worlds. I see them as the same language.


I’m a Software Engineer with over four years of experience, but my journey didn't start with just a keyboard... Not at all, it actually started with a fascination for how systems work, both in the market and in the machine. With dual degrees in Computer Science and Finance, I’ve spent my career standing at the intersection of these two fast-paced industries, translating complex financial logic into high-performance reality.


This dual-lens perspective was my secret weapon at Tata Consultancy Services. There, I didn't just write code; I translated intricate financial and manufacturing requirements into scalable digital architectures, ensuring that complex business logic was never lost in translation from the boardroom to the server.


What I Do Best

I specialize in the Java/Spring Boot ecosystem, building the "engine room" of modern web applications, like scalable microservices, robust REST APIs, and the backend systems that keep things running when the stakes are high. On the flip side, I use Angular and JavaScript to build the "cockpit," turning dense financial data into intuitive dashboards that actually make sense to the human eye.


Beyond the Codebase

My passion for building is not only tied to work. If I’m not lost in a great book, practicing a new song on my guitar, or diving into my video-game collection. You’ll likely find me designing and building "mock-up" applications inspired by the franchises I love.


Connect with me on LinkeIn or shoot me a quick email, and let’s build something that matters.
